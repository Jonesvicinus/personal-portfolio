# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tech recruiters, hiring managers, potential collaborators, and fellow engineers. They arrive from a job application, a LinkedIn profile, or a referral. They're deciding in under 60 seconds whether Jones is worth reaching out to, worth hiring, or worth remembering. Audience is not one type — the site must land with both a non-technical recruiter and a senior engineer reviewing the same page.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for Jones Vicinus — a CS student presenting technical depth and professional presence to the world. The site is the pitch. Success means a visitor leaves with a clear, lasting impression of who Jones is as an engineer and person, even if they don't act immediately. Brand recall is the primary outcome.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, professional, spacious. Precise without being cold. Confident without being loud. The voice is direct — no hedging, no filler — and the design earns its space by filling it deliberately rather than densely. Think: a person who knows exactly what they're doing and doesn't need to oversell it.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered or information-dense portfolios that scan like a resume dump
- Warm, cozy, casual personal sites with informal typography and soft palettes
- Generic SaaS-cream templates ("modern but forgettable")
- Overly decorative or whimsical designs that undermine technical credibility
- Layouts that feel timid — too much white space with nothing to hold the eye

## Design Principles

1. **Space is intentional** — Elements fill the viewport with weight and purpose. Whitespace is structure, not absence.
2. **Sharp over soft** — Typography, copy, and visual choices signal precision. No rounded edges for their own sake, no hedging language, no decorative filler.
3. **Motion earns its place** — Animation guides attention and gives the site presence. Every transition should feel considered, not automatic.
4. **One impression, lasting** — Every section contributes to a single coherent persona. Visitors who don't act today should still remember the name.
5. **Confidence without noise** — Strong presence comes from restraint and commitment, not density or decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline. Standard contrast ratios, keyboard navigability, semantic HTML, and focus states. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all non-essential animations.
